FAQs: clarity for your next move

Get clear, direct answers about our agent support, onboarding, and what to expect when you join eXp with Freedom Partners Group.

Apply

What support is provided to agents?

Agents receive weekly coaching, mentorship, and proven systems to help build a reliable, sustainable business.

Who should consider joining?

We welcome residential and commercial agents, teams, investors, and brokerages looking for structure, accountability, and a community inside eXp Realty.

What is the onboarding process?

Onboarding features step-by-step training, resources, and guidance to help you start confidently.

How is your approach unique?

We focus on practical skills, consistent execution, and long-term growth — no hype or generic advice.

Do I need real estate experience to join?

Not necessarily. We support agents at every stage — from newly licensed agents who want a real structure from day one, to experienced agents who are ready for more mentorship and fewer things falling through the cracks.

What tools and technology do you provide?

You'll get access to modern systems for lead management, marketing, and daily operations — built to cut down busywork so you can spend more time with clients.

How is mentorship structured?

Every agent is paired with an experienced mentor for ongoing, one-on-one guidance, not just a welcome call. You'll have someone to review deals, answer questions, and help you navigate challenges as they come up.

What does compensation look like?

Compensation is structured to reward production and reflect the support you receive along the way. We'll walk you through how it works, including growth incentives and additional opportunities, during your application call.

Can I bring my own clients and leads?

Absolutely. Your existing relationships and pipeline are yours to bring with you. On top of that, you'll have access to our lead generation systems and marketing support to keep growing your business.

What is the culture like at Freedom Partners Group?

We're built around collaboration, accountability, and genuine support, not internal competition. Agents share ideas, celebrate wins together, and help each other solve problems, so you're never building alone.
